# Product Decisions

Research date: 2026-05-06

## App Shape

- Mobile-first public bakery site for Instagram and WhatsApp traffic.
- No database in v1.
- No card payments or customer account system.
- Inquiry form produces a copyable summary and can send email only when Resend environment variables are present.
- Internal `/launch-kit` is noindex and founder-facing.

## Copy Decisions

- Use "Brampton home kitchen" and "pickup details shared after booking."
- Use "not halal certified" instead of "halal cakes."
- Mention no alcohol or pork-derived ingredients as ingredient intent, not certification.
- Do not invent testimonials, customer photos, exact address, inspection claims, or licensing claims.

## Pricing Decisions

- Use introductory ranges because Meera is launching and needs portfolio-building room.
- Show estimates as guidance, not checkout totals.
- Add-ons are priced separately to protect time spent on florals, berries, fondant names, and premium fillings.

## UX Decisions

- Keep primary actions simple: view menu, start quote, copy summary, email inquiry.
- Require acknowledgements for notice, allergens, private pickup address, and no certification.
- Use placeholder portfolio cards that are explicitly labelled as launch placeholders.
- Keep launch-kit scripts practical and private-facing.
