# Legal And Compliance Notes

Research date: 2026-05-06

This is product guidance for the launch site, not legal advice. Meera should confirm obligations directly with Peel Public Health and the City of Brampton before taking paid orders.

## Official Sources

- Peel Public Health: [Starting a home-based food business](https://peelregion.ca/health/protecting-your-health/starting-home-based-food-business)
- Ontario: [O. Reg. 493/17: Food Premises](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/170493)
- City of Brampton: [Home Occupations](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/residents/By-Law-Enforcement/pages/home-occupations.aspx)
- Canada Food and Drug Regulations: [Halal claim rule, B.01.050](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/c.r.c.%2C_c._870/FullText.html)
- CFIA: [List of ingredients and allergens on food labels](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/list-ingredients-and-allergens)

## Implications For This Site

- Do not imply the business is licensed, inspected, approved, or certified unless Meera has written evidence and the exact claim is reviewed.
- Do not post the home address publicly. Brampton home occupation guidance points to residential-business restrictions, and pickup coordination should stay private.
- Do not use "halal" as a product claim unless the certifying body is identified. Safer launch wording: "No alcohol or pork-derived ingredients are intentionally used. Not halal certified."
- Keep allergen copy conservative. CFIA guidance requires allergen sources in ingredient/allergen labelling contexts; the public site should avoid "allergen-free" and state the home kitchen may handle common allergens.
- Treat refrigerated/high-risk products as hold-for-later until Meera confirms equipment, sanitation, certification, and inspection requirements. Peel distinguishes low-risk foods from high/moderate-risk foods that need time-temperature controls.

## Customer-Safe Copy Rules

- Allowed: "prepared from a Brampton home kitchen," "pickup details shared after booking," "not halal certified," "common allergens may be present."
- Avoid: "licensed home bakery," "Peel approved," "halal cakes," "nut-free," "allergen-free," "same-day custom cakes."
- Use written confirmation for date, pickup, price, allergens, design, and deposit.
