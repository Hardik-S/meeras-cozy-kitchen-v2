# Menu Risk Notes

Research date: 2026-05-06

## Lower-Risk Launch Items

Peel describes low-risk foods as non-hazardous foods that do not require time and temperature control, with examples such as cookies, muffins, breads, brownies, and trail mix. For a cake launch, this supports a conservative first menu built around stable buttercream-style cakes and cupcakes, subject to Meera confirming the exact recipes and handling process.

## Hold-For-Later Items

Keep these out of the public offer until Meera confirms the operational and regulatory path:

- Fresh cream cakes
- Custard fillings
- Cheesecakes
- Cream pies
- Meat, seafood, rice, pasta, or potato items
- Wedding or tiered cakes
- Delivery
- Certified halal or allergen-free claims

## Product UX Decision

The quote builder should let customers request common cakes, cupcakes, and dessert boxes only. It can show a "not offered yet" section for transparency, but it should not let customers submit high-risk products as selectable menu items.
