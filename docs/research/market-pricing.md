# Market Pricing Notes

Research date: 2026-05-06

The launch prices should be credible for Brampton/GTA customers without overpromising expert-level custom work before Meera has a portfolio.

## Source Checks

- Cakes n Bloom lists one-tier buttercream cakes starting around $90-$180 depending on size, with 6 inch at $120-$140 and 8 inch at $150-$180, plus notes that design changes final price: <https://cakesnbloom.com/pages/faq>
- Bella's Custom Cakes in Mississauga lists 6 inch cakes from $140 and 8 inch cakes from $180, with customization and fondant add-ons: <https://www.bellascustomcakes.com/pricing>
- Search results for Brampton-area bakers show custom order prices vary by toppers, design, and size; local grocery cakes are much cheaper and should not be used as the custom-home-baker benchmark.

## Launch Price Positioning

Current app pricing intentionally sits below the higher custom-bakery references while still avoiding grocery-store anchoring:

- 6 inch round cake: $58-$68
- 8 inch round cake: $88-$100
- 10 inch round cake: $128-$150
- Cupcake dozen: $34-$44
- Dessert box: $38-$48
- Add-ons: $5-$18 depending on finish

These are introductory launch ranges. The site should make clear that a final quote depends on size, flavour, design, add-ons, timeline, and ingredient needs.

## Price Increase Trigger

Raise base prices after 5 to 8 successful orders with real photos and clearer prep time. The first increase should move 6 inch and 8 inch cakes closer to the local custom reference range while preserving a simple, narrower launch menu.
